What is measured and what is projected
--------------------------------------
* Measured (this machine, this load, 251-bar windows): ms per signal call per pair, ms per
  simulated window per pair, ms per gate-mask bar per direction. These are environment-
  specific; a rerun on the same machine gives visibly different per-pair numbers (compare
  the two runs below).
* Projected, not measured: everything in hours. The history is synthetic: 4280 bars/symbol
  (--bars-per-symbol default) on business days ending 2026-11-16, an ASSUMED end (today; the
  fixture directory has no export manifest). With --bars-dir pointing at an
  export_tuning_bars.py export, the probe uses the manifest's fixed end date instead.
  Universe sizes come from the current configuration (trade 498, trade + fire-rate 512,
  sample 110), not from stored history.
* The re-check is counted at one distinct winner per fold, every fold winning: a cold-cache
  upper bound. Ordinary winners only on the eligible bars of their fold's test-entry window
  (from the 60th bar); setup winners and the final parameters on full series; the final
  parameters on every trade- and fire-rate-universe symbol (evidence-only symbols included).
* Not claimed: that the 688 h or 540 h figures quoted for the real J1 run have been measured
  down. Nothing here ran on real history. The sample-grid signal work (full history, all
  valid combinations on 110 sample symbols) is unchanged by Task 1 and is now the dominant
  residual; simulation and gate masks are smaller residuals. All hours are projections,
  not runtime guarantees.
