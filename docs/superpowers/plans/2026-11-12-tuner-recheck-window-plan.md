## Observed mechanism and locked invariants

`report.full_universe_recheck` currently prewarms full signal series for every distinct fold winner and final params across every symbol, then repeats simulations for cost/stress. `simulate_window` retains lead/exit bars, but `simulate_trades` reads `signals[i]` only after its entry-start/end guards. Ordinary `signals.signal_series` computes each independent trailing-window signal using original absolute index/warm-up rules. `setup_series` carries pending setups and ages across bars; it cannot be window-truncated.

The numerical judge is full-series versus optimized **exact** equality of ordered trades, statistics, fire rates, per-symbol fires, boundary/end-dropped counts and adoption verdicts at both costs. No approximate tolerance, golden-pin changes, simulator edits, strategy-engine edits or baseline-result regeneration to hide drift. Python 3.11 only. Sample-grid selection and final-parameter fire/heldout series remain full. The long-held boundary/entry semantics, `_LEAD_BARS`, setup age, event guards, hold days and commissions remain unchanged.
