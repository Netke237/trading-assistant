How do I read these curves?

Blue follows the example strategy. Dashed grey represents staying invested in the underlying stock. Both begin at 0% return, equivalent to an index of 100. A value of +20% means 100 has become 120. Hide holding to inspect smaller strategy movements. The red panel shows how far the strategy has fallen from its own previous high. Calculation basis & comparison limits

Equity: proposed default retains the app's recorded equity, including open-position mark-to-market. TradingView's documented performance chart uses closed-trade cumulative P&L; these definitions must remain distinct.

Benchmark: a long buy-and-hold comparison, excluding fees. Actual commission, allocation, dividends, exposure and price-adjustment treatment must be disclosed from recorded engine data in the application. None is inferred by this preview.

Drawdown: computed from the synthetic strategy index relative to its running peak. It is not the engine's intrabar maximum-drawdown metric.

Display ranges: change which dates are visible, not the test period or starting capital. No currency conversion is offered because currency is not recorded.
